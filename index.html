The Triple Captain Duel

Two managers pulled the same chip in the same week. Cap, sitting top, put the Triple Captain on Cherki. Diana, sitting 5th, put hers on Haaland. Same gamble, very different nights.

Cherki gave Cap 3 points, tripled to 9. Haaland gave Diana 9 points, tripled to 27. That 18 point gap is almost the whole margin between them this week. Diana scored 78, Cap 58, and she jumps to 2nd, 19 behind the leader.

Six managers wore the Haaland armband, but only Diana tripled it. Tyler quietly had the best pick of all with Isak, 13 raw for 26. Cap keeps the top spot with 232. Diana, Chris and Ryan sit within 29 points. This race just got interesting!
