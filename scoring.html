Multipliers and Extras
Captain
2x points
Your captain's score doubles. The vice steps in if the captain doesn't play.
The Bench
Scores nothing
Your four bench players earn you nothing unless a starter doesn't play. Then the bench sub steps in, in order.
Free Transfers
1 per week
Skip a week and it rolls over. You can bank up to 5.
Extra Transfers
-4 each
Moves beyond your free transfers cost 4 points apiece, off the top of your score.
The Chips, 2026/27

Four one-week power-ups. You get each one twice: once to play by GW19, once from GW20 on (Wildcard and Free Hit open in GW2). One chip per week, and an unused first-half chip vanishes after GW19.

Wildcard
Rebuild for free
Make as many transfers as you like that week with no point hits. The new squad stays.
Free Hit
One-week loaner
Unlimited free transfers for one week only. Next week your old squad comes back.
Bench Boost
All 15 count
Your four bench players score too, just for that week.
Triple Captain
3x points
Your captain scores triple instead of double, one week only.
